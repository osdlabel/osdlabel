# Landscape

Other viewers and annotation tools: their architecture, and where each struggles.

## The gap osdlabel can fill

Apart from imaging libraries such as Cornerstone3D, Niivue and vtk.js, every tool that handles time or 3D today is a deployed application: OHIF, CVAT, Label Studio, V7, Labelbox, Supervisely, Encord. The two embeddable OpenSeadragon annotation libraries, Annotorious 3.9.5 and Annota 0.11.1, have no notion of a frame, slice or time. No tool surveyed decodes video frame-exactly in the browser with WebCodecs, although Chrome 94, Firefox 130 and Safari 16.4 all ship it.

The one embeddable renderer for volumes, Niivue 1.0, now also ships deep-zoom slide viewing (with polygon and polyline vectors) and 16 annotation tools for volume slices. osdlabel will not win on rendering. It can win as the annotation layer: typed immutable model, contexts and constraints, framework bindings, DOM decorations, serialization and adapters.

Fact-checked on Oct 24: an independent agent re-verified the 11 load-bearing claims on this tab against npm tarballs, source files and issue pages. None was refuted. Four details were corrected and are reflected below: Niivue's release-candidate version, the scope of its slide tools, Annota's licence, and Label Studio's default frame rate.

## What the field agrees on

- **Tracks.** A track is a set of keyframes plus explicit absence ranges. In-between frames are interpolated, never stored.
- **Stacks are 2.5D.** CT and MR are annotated as 2D shapes pinned to a slice along a plane. Only Cornerstone3D and Supervisely's 3D masks store true patient coordinates.
- **Scale needs two layers.** A fast read-only layer for thousands to millions of objects, and an editing layer for the few being changed.
- **Interactive models run as servers for video and 3D.** SAM 2's own demo, nnInteractive, MedSAM2 and MONAI Label all keep the model on a GPU server with a session; the browser sends prompts and receives masks.

## The WebGL context ceiling everyone works around

Chromium and WebKit keep at most 16 live WebGL contexts per page and evict the oldest beyond that. Cornerstone3D's pool of 7 contexts exists for this reason; Niivue lets many views share one canvas. osdlabel's pinned OpenSeadragon 5.0.1 creates a WebGL context per viewer: measured, 20 viewers left 4 cells blank, so a full 4×4 grid, the default maximum, sits exactly at the limit, and hosts can already configure larger grids. OpenSeadragon 6.1.1 recovers lost contexts but still evicts them repeatedly (44 warnings in one redraw of 20 viewers).
